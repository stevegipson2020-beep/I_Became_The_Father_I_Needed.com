From Chapter One: The Man I Never Had

“I had a father, but I didn’t really have a father figure.”

I grew up wanting the kind of guidance that many children never have to question—someone to show me what a father was supposed to be, how a man was supposed to love his children, and how to keep going when life gets hard.

Instead, I learned from what was missing.

I learned how it feels to be surrounded by people and still feel completely alone. I learned how to depend on myself. And I learned that pain can either break you or become the reason you build something better.

This is the story of how a man who never felt fully chosen became a father determined to make sure his children always knew they were loved.

“I don’t just want to be remembered as a man who survived. I want to be remembered as a man who built something from what he survived.”

I Became the Father I Needed is a story of fatherhood, healing, foster care, adoption, resilience, and the power of showing up—even when life gives you every reason to walk away.
